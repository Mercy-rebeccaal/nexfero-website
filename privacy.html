Compliance & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: 2026

Summary: NexFero Innovations ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. We collect minimal personal data solely to communicate regarding digital engineering projects and never sell, rent, or trade client information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an inquiry, we collect:

  • Direct Inquiries: Your full name, email address, phone number, organization name, requested services, project budget range, and project description.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, browser type, device telemetry, referral URLs, and pages viewed, collected via server logs to maintain system security and uptime.
  • Direct Communications: Any messages, feedback, or technical requirements transmitted via our contact forms or email to nexferoinnovations@gmail.com.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

The information collected is strictly used for:

  • Reviewing technical project inquiries and formulating architectural estimates.
  • Communicating with you regarding project proposals, milestones, and technical deliveries.
  • Ensuring network security, detecting malicious intrusion attempts, and preventing fraud.
  • Complying with statutory and legal obligations in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

3. Data Confidentiality & Client Intellectual Property

At NexFero Innovations, we treat all project discussions, system architectures, proprietary formulas, and business ideas submitted by clients with absolute confidentiality. We routinely sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to detailed technical engagements.

4. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 for resting data) and strict principle-of-least-privilege access controls. While no digital system can guarantee 100% impenetrability, our zero-trust engineering methodologies minimize risks of unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Cookies & Local Analytics

Our website uses minimal essential functional cookies and browser storage (such as localStorage) to store your active theme preferences and preserve your inquiry reference status. We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

6. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to any personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate records, or request complete deletion of your submission records. To exercise these rights, email us at nexferoinnovations@gmail.com.
